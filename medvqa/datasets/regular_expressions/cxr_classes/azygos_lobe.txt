
    # 1. Standard phrasing (optional hyphen; optional forming/creating gap)
    # Matches: "azygos lobe", "azygous fissure", "azygos accessory lobe",
    # "azigos vein fissure", "azygos pseudofissure", "azygos pseudo-fissure",
    # "azygos-fissure", "azygos vein forming a fissure"
    \b(?:azyg|azig)\w*(?:[\s-]+(?:vein|accessory))?(?:[\s-]+(?:form(?:ing|ed)|creat(?:ing|ed))(?:[\s-]+(?:a|an|the))?)?[\s-]+(?:pseudo[\s-]*)?(?:lobe|fissure)
    |
    # 2. Inverted phrasing (of/from; formed/created by — not locative "lobe by")
    # Matches: "lobe of the azygos", "fissure of the azygos",
    # "pseudofissure of the azygos", "accessory fissure from the azygos",
    # "fissure formed by the azygos"
    \b(?:pseudo[\s-]*)?(?:lobe|fissure)\s+(?:of|from|(?:formed|created)\s+by)\s+(?:the\s+)?(?:azyg|azig)\w*
