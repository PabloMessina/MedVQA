Adequate chest radiograph with none of the modeled abnormal findings or support devices/hardware present.
Full NLI prompt
According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show an overall normal or unremarkable study with no radiographic abnormality. Presence is supported by an explicit overall assessment that the chest radiograph is normal, unremarkable, or without abnormality; “no acute cardiopulmonary process” or “no active cardiopulmonary disease” supports it only when no current acute or chronic radiographic abnormality is described. Clear lungs and absence of focal consolidation, pleural effusion, or pneumothorax support this concept only as part of an overall normal assessment. Any current radiographic abnormality precludes it, including acute or chronic, stable, benign, healed, or postoperative findings (e.g., scoliosis, kyphosis, emphysema, fibrosis, scarring, calcified granuloma, fracture sequela), as well as explicitly noted anatomic variants (e.g., azygos lobe). Support devices and device-related findings also preclude it, even when uncomplicated and appropriately positioned. A nondiagnostic or severely limited exam does not support this concept. History, indication, comparison-only statements, or absence of selected abnormalities alone do not establish an overall normal current study.
