According to the report, the current CXR image(s) shows evidence of osteopenia when it describes osteopenia, osteopenic bones, osseous demineralization, or decreased, low, reduced, or diminished mineralization or radiographic density of visible thoracic bones, whether generalized or regional. Osteoporosis, osteoporotic change, and clear colloquial descriptions such as “washed-out bones” also support this finding when described on the current radiograph. Do not infer osteopenia from fractures, compression deformities, kyphosis, scoliosis, degenerative changes, osteophytes, lytic lesions, osteomyelitis, osteonecrosis, or osteoarthritis alone. Base the assessment on current imaging findings; history, indication, prior-only findings, and DXA or DEXA results alone do not support current radiographic presence.
