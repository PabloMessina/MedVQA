According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show a cardiac, cardiomediastinal, or mediastinal silhouette that is enlarged, widened, or larger than normal. This umbrella finding is supported by explicit cardiomegaly or cardiac-silhouette enlargement; by explicit enlargement or widening of the cardiomediastinum, mediastinum, or mediastinal contour; and by aortic enlargement or ectasia that enlarges the cardiomediastinal contour. A mediastinal mass, adenopathy, or pulmonary-artery enlargement supports this finding only when it is stated to widen or enlarge the mediastinal or cardiomediastinal silhouette. Do not infer this finding from mediastinal shift, hilar abnormality, pulmonary artery enlargement, aortic tortuosity or unfolding without enlargement, or apparent enlargement attributed solely to projection, rotation, or poor inspiration. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
