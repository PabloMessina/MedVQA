According to the report, the current CXR image(s) shows an overall normal or unremarkable study with no radiographic abnormality. Presence is supported by an explicit overall assessment that the chest radiograph is normal, unremarkable, or without abnormality; “no acute cardiopulmonary process” or “no active cardiopulmonary disease” supports it only when no current acute or chronic radiographic abnormality is described. Clear lungs and absence of focal consolidation, pleural effusion, or pneumothorax support this concept only as part of an overall normal assessment. Any current pathologic finding, including a chronic, stable, benign, or healed abnormality such as scoliosis, kyphosis, emphysema, fibrosis, scarring, or a calcified granuloma, precludes it. Uncomplicated, appropriately positioned support devices may coexist with this concept when the study is otherwise explicitly normal, but device malposition or complication precludes it. History, indication, comparison-only statements, or absence of selected abnormalities alone do not establish an overall normal current study.
