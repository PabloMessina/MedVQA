According to the report, the current CXR image(s) shows evidence of pulmonary fibrosis when it explicitly describes fibrosis, fibrotic or fibrosing change, parenchymal or pleuroparenchymal scarring, fibrocalcific or fibroatelectatic scarring, honeycombing, traction bronchiectasis or bronchiolectasis, architectural distortion, or names a fibrotic interstitial lung disease or pattern such as UIP, IPF, or fibrotic NSIP as a current imaging finding. Do not infer pulmonary fibrosis from a disease name or etiology alone; sarcoidosis, cystic fibrosis, asbestos exposure, tuberculosis, connective tissue disease, or prior radiation requires a current fibrotic or scarring finding. Generic reticular opacities, interstitial prominence or thickening, interstitial markings, linear opacities, or chronic lung disease do not support fibrosis unless attributed to fibrosis or scarring, and edema- or heart-failure-related interstitial findings do not qualify. Isolated pleural thickening, pleural plaques, fibrothorax, or non-traction bronchiectasis does not support pulmonary fibrosis without parenchymal fibrotic or scarring change. Base the assessment on current imaging findings rather than history, indication, or prior-only findings.
