According to the report, the current CXR image(s) shows one or more pulmonary or intrathoracic nodal granulomas, usually discrete calcified or noncalcified nodular foci. An explicit granuloma, granulomata, granulomatous nodule, calcified granuloma, Ghon focus or complex, or Ranke complex described on the current image supports this finding. The generic phrase “sequelae of prior” or “healed granulomatous disease” alone does not support a current granuloma unless the report also identifies a discrete granuloma, granulomatous nodule, or named granulomatous focus or complex. Do not infer granuloma from tuberculosis, sarcoidosis, histoplasmosis, another fungal infection, or granulomatosis with polyangiitis alone, or from a nonspecific calcified pulmonary nodule or lymph node, fibrocalcific or apical scarring, pleural calcification, or miliary pattern without granuloma or granulomatous wording. Base the assessment on current imaging; history- or prior-only mentions and resolved or absent granulomas do not support current presence, whereas an explicitly stable or unchanged granuloma remains current.
