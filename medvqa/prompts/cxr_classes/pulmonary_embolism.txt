Include acute, subacute, chronic, segmental, subsegmental, or massive PE, chronic thromboembolic pulmonary disease or hypertension (CTEPD/CTEPH), and explicitly diagnosed nonthrombotic pulmonary emboli such as septic, fat, air, tumor, cement, or foreign-body emboli. Base the assessment on findings attributed to the current image; history, indication, and prior- or other-modality-only findings do not support current presence.
