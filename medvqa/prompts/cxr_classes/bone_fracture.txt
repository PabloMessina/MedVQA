According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show one or more fractures or visible fracture sequelae involving bones within the field of view. Explicit fractures of the ribs, clavicle, scapula, sternum, proximal humerus, vertebrae, or another visible osseous structure support this umbrella finding. Include acute, subacute, healing, healed, old, and remote fractures when currently visible, as well as callus, fracture deformity, nonunion, or malunion explicitly attributed to a fracture. Pathologic, traumatic, and iatrogenic fractures also qualify when currently identified. Site-specific findings such as rib fracture, clavicle fracture, vertebral compression fracture, and radiographically identified flail chest support this finding. Do not infer a fracture from history or indication alone, degenerative change, osteopenia, scoliosis, lytic or metastatic lesions, sternotomy, osteotomy, or fixation hardware without a stated fracture or fracture sequela. Exclude broken or fractured devices, leads, wires, and catheters. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
