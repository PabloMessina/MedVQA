According to the report, the current CXR image(s) shows one or more fractures involving bones visible on chest radiography. Explicit fractures of the ribs, clavicle, scapula, sternum, proximal humerus or shoulder, vertebrae or spine, or another thoracic osseous structure support this umbrella finding. Include acute, subacute, healing, healed, old, and remote fractures, as well as callus, fracture deformity, nonunion, or malunion when explicitly attributable to a fracture. Pathologic, traumatic, and iatrogenic or postoperative fractures also qualify when currently identified. Subtypes such as rib fracture, clavicle fracture, vertebral compression fracture, and flail chest support this finding. Do not infer a fracture from history or indication alone, degenerative change, osteopenia, scoliosis, lytic or metastatic lesions, sternotomy, osteotomy, or fixation hardware without a stated fracture or fracture sequela.
