According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show mediastinal widening, meaning generalized widening of the mediastinal contour beyond that expected for the projection and patient positioning. Explicit mediastinal widening, a widened mediastinum, or generalized enlargement or widening of the mediastinal contour supports this finding when attributed to the current radiographs. Explicit widening of a named mediastinal region, such as the superior mediastinum, also supports this finding. Do not infer mediastinal widening from cardiomegaly, cardiac-silhouette enlargement, or nonspecific enlargement of the cardiomediastinal silhouette alone; from aortic enlargement, ectasia, tortuosity, or unfolding alone; from a focal mediastinal mass or localized contour bulge alone; or from hilar enlargement or adenopathy alone. Unqualified "mediastinal enlargement" is insufficient if it refers to a focal lesion or does not establish widening of the mediastinal contour. Do not count apparent mediastinal widening that the report attributes solely to portable AP technique, magnification, rotation, or positioning. If true mediastinal widening remains suspected after accounting for technique, apply the corresponding hedged label. Findings described only on CT, only in clinical history, or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
