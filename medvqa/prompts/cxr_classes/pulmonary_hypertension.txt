According to the report, the current CXR image(s) shows evidence of pulmonary hypertension when the report explicitly states or hedges a current diagnosis of pulmonary hypertension (PH), pulmonary arterial or artery hypertension (PAH), pulmonary venous hypertension, PHTN, chronic thromboembolic pulmonary hypertension (CTEPH), or pre-capillary, post-capillary, or combined pulmonary hypertension. A current appearance explicitly attributed to pulmonary hypertension also supports the finding, including enlarged central pulmonary arteries with peripheral vascular pruning when interpreted as evidence of pulmonary hypertension. Do not infer pulmonary hypertension from enlarged or prominent pulmonary arteries, right-heart enlargement, cardiomegaly, pulmonary vascular congestion, cephalization, or pulmonary edema alone. Cor pulmonale supports this finding only when pulmonary hypertension is also stated or identified as its cause, and systemic hypertension or bare “HTN” does not qualify. Base the assessment on current imaging findings; history, indication, and prior-only findings do not support current presence.
