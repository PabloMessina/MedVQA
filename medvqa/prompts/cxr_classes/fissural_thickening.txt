According to the report, the current CXR image(s) shows thickening or scarring of an interlobar pulmonary fissure. Explicit thickening, thickening-related prominence or accentuation, scarring, fibrosis, sclerosis, or plaques involving the major, minor, oblique, or horizontal fissure supports this finding, as does interlobar pleural thickening clearly localized to a fissure. Do not infer fissural thickening from a normally visible or incidentally identified fissure, nonspecific fissural prominence, an accessory or azygos fissure, generic pleural thickening or plaques, pleural effusion, or pulmonary scarring without fissural localization. Fissural or interlobar fluid and a fissural pseudotumor support Fissural Effusion rather than this finding unless separate thickening or scarring not attributable solely to fluid is also described; overlap with Pleural Thickening is appropriate when chronic pleural thickening, scarring, or fibrosis is explicitly localized to the fissure. Base the assessment on current imaging; history- or prior-only mentions and resolved or absent thickening do not support current presence, whereas explicitly stable or unchanged fissural thickening remains current.
