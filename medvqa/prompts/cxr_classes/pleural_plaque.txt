According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show one or more pleural plaques, meaning discrete focal areas of pleural thickening with plaque-like morphology, whether calcified or noncalcified. Explicit pleural plaque or plaques, or explicitly plaque-like pleural thickening, supports this finding when attributed to the current radiographs. This includes calcified, noncalcified, diaphragmatic, and asbestos-related pleural plaques. Do not infer pleural plaques from generic pleural thickening, pleural scarring, pleural fibrosis, fibrothorax, pleural calcification, apical pleural capping, asbestos exposure, or pleural effusion alone when plaque or plaque-like morphology is not stated. A pleural-based opacity or mass also does not establish a pleural plaque unless characterized as a plaque. Findings described only on CT, only in clinical history, or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
