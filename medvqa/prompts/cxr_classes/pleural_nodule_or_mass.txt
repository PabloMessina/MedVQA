According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show one or more nodular or mass-like pleural tissue lesions. Presence is supported by an explicit pleural nodule, mass, nodularity, mammillation, implant, or deposit, including pleural metastases. Mesothelioma, solitary fibrous tumor, or another pleural neoplasm supports this finding when described as forming a current pleural nodular or mass-like lesion. A pleural-based or extrapleural-appearing mass qualifies only when the report identifies pleural origin or involvement; pleural contact, an extrapleural sign, or location at the pleural–chest-wall interface alone is insufficient. Exclude intrapulmonary, chest-wall, and other extrapleural lesions without stated pleural involvement; smooth or diffuse pleural thickening, plaques, scarring, calcification, or apical capping without a nodular or mass-like component; and loculated fluid, empyema, or a pleural fluid pseudotumor without a separate tissue lesion. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
