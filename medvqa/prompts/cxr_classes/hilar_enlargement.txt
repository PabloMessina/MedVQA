According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show abnormal enlargement or prominence of one or both pulmonary hila. A hilum, hila, or hilar silhouette explicitly described as enlarged, abnormally prominent, bulky, full, widened, lobulated, convex, or increased in size supports this finding. Enlargement caused by dilated central pulmonary arteries, hilar adenopathy or enlarged hilar nodes, or a hilar mass, lesion, tumor, or opacity also supports it when the abnormality involves or expands the hilar silhouette. A perihilar abnormality supports hilar enlargement only when stated to involve or enlarge the hilum. Do not infer this finding from a normal hilar mention, isolated hilar calcification, increased hilar density without enlargement, displacement from volume loss, pulmonary vascular congestion, cephalization, redistribution, edema, pulmonary hypertension, mediastinal widening, or non-hilar adenopathy alone. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
