Supporting findings include explicit pulmonary emphysema, emphysematous change or destruction, bullous emphysema, or lungs described as emphysematous. Exclude subcutaneous or soft-tissue emphysema, pneumomediastinum or mediastinal emphysema, and pulmonary interstitial emphysema. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
