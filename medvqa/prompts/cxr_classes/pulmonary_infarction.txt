According to the report, the current CXR image(s) shows evidence of pulmonary infarction when it explicitly states or hedges pulmonary or lung infarction, pulmonary ischemia, lung tissue necrosis, Hampton hump, or the melting sign. A peripheral, juxtapleural, or wedge-shaped opacity or consolidation, and other nonspecific opacity, atelectasis, pneumonia, or hemorrhage morphology, supports this finding only when the report explicitly links that morphology to pulmonary infarction, pulmonary ischemia, or pulmonary embolism; pulmonary embolism without a linked current lung morphology does not by itself support infarction. Do not infer pulmonary infarction from myocardial infarction, heart attack, vertebral wedge compression fracture, or infarction or avascular necrosis of another organ or tissue. Base the assessment on current imaging findings rather than history, indication, or prior-only findings.
