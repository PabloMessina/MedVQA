According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show a thoracic hernia or herniation. This umbrella finding includes hiatal or hiatus hernia, including sliding, paraesophageal, rolling, mixed, and type I–IV forms; diaphragmatic hernia, including Bochdalek or Morgagni hernia; intrathoracic stomach or gastric herniation; abdominal viscera herniating into the thorax; traumatic diaphragmatic hernia or rupture with current herniation; and lung tissue herniating beyond the thoracic cage. A retrocardiac opacity, mass, or air-fluid level supports this finding only when attributed to a hiatal or diaphragmatic hernia. Do not infer hernia from reflux, an elevated hemidiaphragm, eventration, diaphragmatic injury or rupture without herniation, or nonspecific intrathoracic gas or bowel-like opacity. Exclude expected postoperative anatomy such as gastric pull-up, epiphrenic diverticulum, phrenic ampulla, and other retrocardiac mimics unless a separate current or recurrent hernia is described. Findings described only in clinical history, as a prior repair, or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
