According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show a mediastinal mass, meaning a focal mediastinal soft-tissue opacity or contour abnormality characterized as a mass or mass-like lesion. Explicit mediastinal mass, mass-like mediastinal opacity, nodal mass, or focal mediastinal contour bulge characterized as a mass supports this finding when attributed to the current radiographs. Do not infer a mediastinal mass from a nonspecific mediastinal opacity or contour prominence without mass or mass-like framing; from diffuse mediastinal widening or generalized mediastinal enlargement alone; or from mediastinal or hilar adenopathy alone, even if bulky, unless it is described as a nodal mass or conglomerate mass. Do not infer it from cardiomegaly, aortic enlargement or unfolding, thyroid enlargement, or a pulmonary or pleural mass alone. A lesion merely projected over the mediastinum is insufficient unless the report localizes it to the mediastinum. Findings described only on CT, only in clinical history, or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
