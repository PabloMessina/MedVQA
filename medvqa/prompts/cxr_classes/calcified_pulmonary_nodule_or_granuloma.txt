According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show one or more calcified intrapulmonary nodules or granulomas. Presence is supported by an explicit calcified pulmonary or lung nodule, calcified pulmonary granuloma or granulomata, calcified granulomatous nodule, calcified Ghon focus, or Ranke complex. A Ranke complex supports this finding because it includes a healed, calcified intrapulmonary Ghon focus. A Ghon focus or Ghon complex supports this finding only when its intrapulmonary focus is explicitly described as calcified or calcific. A pulmonary granuloma or nodule otherwise qualifies only when calcification or calcific content is explicitly stated. Do not infer this finding from a noncalcified nodule, mass, or granuloma; from tuberculosis, sarcoidosis, histoplasmosis, or another granulomatous disease alone; or from fibrocalcific or apical scarring, pleural calcification, or calcified lymph nodes without a calcified intrapulmonary nodule or granuloma. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
