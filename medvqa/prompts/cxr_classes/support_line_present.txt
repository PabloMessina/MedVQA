According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show one or more therapeutic support lines. Explicit wording of a currently visible medical line, vascular access line, or intravascular catheter supports this finding. A named support line—such as a central venous catheter, PICC, dialysis catheter, pulmonary artery catheter, or implanted venous port—also supports this umbrella finding. Do not infer a support line from an unspecified "catheter" unless the report establishes that it is an intravascular or line-type support device. Do not infer this finding from an endotracheal, enteric, or chest tube; pacemaker or ICD leads alone; surgical hardware; ECG electrodes or monitoring stickers; external oxygen tubing; clothing artifacts; or nontherapeutic foreign bodies. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
