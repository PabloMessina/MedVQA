According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show pneumothorax, meaning pleural-space air or gas. Presence is supported by a current pneumothorax, PTX, or aerothorax of any size or laterality, including simple, apical, loculated, interlobar or interfissural, spontaneous, traumatic, iatrogenic, postoperative, ex vacuo, or tension pneumothorax, as well as hydropneumothorax, hemopneumothorax, or pyopneumothorax because each contains pleural air. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
