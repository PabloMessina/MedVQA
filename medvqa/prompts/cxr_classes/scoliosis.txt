Purely anterior-posterior kyphosis or lordosis does not support scoliosis unless combined with scoliosis. Do not infer scoliosis from spinal fusion hardware, chest-wall deformity, or spinal asymmetry alone without an abnormal lateral curvature or explicit scoliosis, and exclude apparent curvature attributed to rotation or positioning. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
