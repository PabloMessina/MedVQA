According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show cardiac or valvular calcification, meaning calcific density localized to a native cardiac valve or annulus, the myocardium, pericardium, coronary arteries, or another cardiac structure. Explicit calcification or calcific deposits involving a native cardiac valve or valvular annulus—including the mitral, aortic, tricuspid, or pulmonic valve or annulus—or involving the myocardium, pericardium, or coronary arteries supports this finding when attributed to the current radiographs. Do not infer this finding from calcification of the aortic wall, root, arch, or descending aorta, or from an atherosclerotic aorta alone; these belong to aortic calcification. A prosthetic cardiac valve, annuloplasty device, or other cardiac hardware does not establish this finding unless separate native cardiac, valvular, annular, myocardial, pericardial, or coronary calcification is stated. Nonspecific calcification projected over or near the cardiac silhouette is insufficient unless the report localizes it to one of these structures. Findings described only on CT, only in clinical history, or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
