According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show displacement of the mediastinum or central thoracic structures. Presence is supported when the mediastinum, heart, or cardiomediastinal silhouette or contour is explicitly shifted, deviated, displaced, pushed, or pulled from its expected position, including toward volume loss or away from mass effect. Tracheal deviation supports mediastinal shift only when mediastinal or cardiomediastinal displacement is also stated; tracheal deviation alone is not equivalent. Chronic, postoperative, or mass-related displacement supports presence only when the displacement itself is stated. Do not infer mediastinal shift from atelectasis, effusion, pneumothorax, mass effect, prominence, widening, distortion, or flattening without explicit displacement, and exclude apparent displacement attributed to rotation, positioning, or scoliosis as well as dextrocardia and situs inversus. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
