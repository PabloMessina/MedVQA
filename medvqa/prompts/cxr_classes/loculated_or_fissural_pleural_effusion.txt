According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show localized pleural fluid that is loculated or collected within an interlobar fissure. Presence is supported by an explicit loculated, encysted, or walled-off pleural effusion or fluid collection; fissural or interlobar pleural fluid; fluid along the major, minor, oblique, or horizontal fissure; or a pleural fluid collection centered on a fissure. A pleural pseudotumor, phantom tumor, or vanishing tumor also supports this finding when described as a current radiographic finding. A lenticular or biconvex fissure-based opacity qualifies only when attributed to pleural fluid. Do not infer this finding from a free-flowing or otherwise generic pleural effusion without stated loculation or fissural location; from fissural thickening, scarring, displacement, prominence, or visibility without fissural fluid; or from pleural thickening alone. Exclude true pulmonary masses or nodules and normal or accessory fissures, including an azygos fissure. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
