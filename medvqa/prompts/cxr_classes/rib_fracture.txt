According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show evidence of one or more rib fractures when an acute, subacute, healing, healed, old, remote, iatrogenic, postoperative, or pathologic rib fracture is explicitly identified on the current exam. Current radiographic sequelae explicitly attributed to rib fracture, including callus formation or fracture deformity, also support this finding. History- or indication-only mentions, including a “known rib fracture,” do not support current presence unless the fracture or its sequela is also described on the current image. Do not infer a rib fracture from an isolated clavicle, sternum, scapula, or spine fracture, or from bony destruction, lytic lesions, or metastases without an explicitly stated rib fracture. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
