According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show one or more calcific deposits within the field of view, including unlocalized calcification and calcification with a stated anatomic site. This umbrella finding includes explicit calcified, calcific, calcification, or calcinosis wording involving the aorta or other vascular structures, pleura or pleural plaques, pulmonary nodules or granulomas, hilar or mediastinal lymph nodes, coronary arteries, cardiac valves, pericardium, costochondral structures, or other soft tissues. Atherosclerotic, atheromatous, or sclerotic change does not by itself establish calcification; it supports this finding only when calcification is explicitly stated. Do not infer calcification from nodules, granulomas, adenopathy, plaques, or chronic granulomatous disease without calcific wording, or from normal bone, osteopenia, or metallic devices such as clips, wires, or pacemakers. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
