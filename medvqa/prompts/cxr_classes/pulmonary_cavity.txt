According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show a pulmonary cavity or cavitary lung lesion. Supporting findings include an explicit pulmonary cavity, cavitation, cavitary or cavitating lesion, lung abscess, excavation or excavated pulmonary lesion, or an intrapulmonary air-fluid or gas-fluid level within a lesion, mass, nodule, consolidation, or parenchymal opacity. Other air-containing lung abnormalities—including emphysema, blebs, bullae, thin-walled cysts, pneumatoceles, honeycombing, and cystic bronchiectasis—do not support this finding unless the report additionally identifies cavitation or a lung abscess. Pseudocavitation does not qualify. Tuberculosis, fungal infection, malignancy, septic emboli, infarction, or pneumonia alone does not establish a cavity without stated cavitation or abscess formation. Skeletal concavities and extrapulmonary air-fluid levels involving the pleura, bowel, or a hiatal hernia do not qualify. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
