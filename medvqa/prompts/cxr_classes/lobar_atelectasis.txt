Supporting findings include explicit lobar, bilobar, or multilobar atelectasis or collapse; complete atelectasis, collapse, or volume loss of a named lobe; complete or total collapse of either lung; an opacified or airless hemithorax with volume loss explicitly attributed to atelectasis or collapse; and a Golden S or Luftsichel sign described as evidence of current lobar collapse. Isolated linear, plate-like, discoid, band-like, subsegmental, segmental, basilar, dependent, rounded, or lingular atelectasis does not support this subtype unless an entire lobe or lung is also involved. Consolidation, pneumonia, lobar opacity, nonspecific volume loss, fissural displacement, or an elevated hemidiaphragm does not establish lobar atelectasis without explicit lobar atelectasis or collapse. Base the assessment on current imaging; history-, indication-, differential-, or prior-only mentions do not support current presence, while stable or unchanged lobar collapse explicitly present on the current exam remains supportive.
