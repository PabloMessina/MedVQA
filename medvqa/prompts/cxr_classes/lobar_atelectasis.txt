According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show evidence of atelectasis or collapse involving an entire lung lobe or multiple lobes. Supporting findings include explicit lobar, bilobar, or multilobar atelectasis or collapse; complete atelectasis, collapse, or volume loss of a named lobe; and a Golden S or Luftsichel sign described as evidence of current lobar collapse. Isolated linear, plate-like, discoid, band-like, subsegmental, segmental, basilar, dependent, rounded, or lingular atelectasis does not support this subtype unless entire-lobe involvement is also stated. Whole-lung or hemithorax collapse alone does not establish lobar atelectasis. Consolidation, pneumonia, lobar opacity, nonspecific volume loss, fissural displacement, or an elevated hemidiaphragm does not establish lobar atelectasis without explicit lobar atelectasis or collapse. Findings described only in clinical history, indication, or differential, or only on a prior examination, do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
