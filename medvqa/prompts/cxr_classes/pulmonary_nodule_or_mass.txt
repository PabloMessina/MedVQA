According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show one or more discrete intrapulmonary nodules or masses. Presence is supported by an explicit pulmonary or lung nodule, mass, micronodule, coin lesion, nodular or mass-like pulmonary opacity, density, or lesion, pulmonary nodularity representing discrete nodules, pulmonary granuloma (calcified or noncalcified), nodular pulmonary metastasis, or a pulmonary tumor or neoplasm when described as a current intrapulmonary imaging finding, whether solitary or multiple. No nodule-versus-mass size cutoff is required. Do not infer this finding from generic opacity, consolidation, infiltrate, atelectasis, interstitial markings, or nonspecific nodularity without a discrete intrapulmonary nodular or mass-like finding. Exclude extrapulmonary masses or nodules (pleural, mediastinal, hilar without intrapulmonary origin, chest-wall, osseous, soft-tissue, skin, nipple), pseudonodules, artifacts, cavities, cysts, and bullae unless a separate intrapulmonary nodule or mass is also stated. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
