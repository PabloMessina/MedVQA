According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show a central pulmonary artery that is enlarged or larger than normal. Explicit enlargement, dilatation, ectasia, aneurysmal change, or increased caliber or diameter of the main pulmonary artery, pulmonary trunk, MPA, or right or left central pulmonary artery supports this finding. “Prominent” or “plump” supports it only when the report identifies abnormal prominence of a central pulmonary artery; generic vascular prominence or potentially physiologic hilar prominence without abnormal pulmonary arterial caliber does not support this finding. Do not infer pulmonary artery enlargement from pulmonary hypertension, cor pulmonale, pulmonary embolism, pulmonary edema, cephalization, vascular redistribution, hilar congestion, indistinct vessels, or hilar enlargement alone. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
