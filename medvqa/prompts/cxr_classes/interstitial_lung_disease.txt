According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show an explicit diagnosis or diagnostic impression of interstitial lung disease (ILD). Presence is supported by current-image wording such as ILD, interstitial lung disease, or an equivalent diagnostic impression, including named interstitial lung diseases (e.g., UIP, NSIP, IPF, or another idiopathic or fibrotic interstitial pneumonia) when presented as a current radiographic diagnosis or impression. Hedged current-image attribution such as possible ILD or “cannot exclude ILD” remains evidence to interpret under the system instructions rather than an exclusion. Do not infer ILD from interstitial opacity, increased interstitial markings, reticular or reticulonodular opacity, septal thickening, honeycombing, fibrosis, scarring, traction bronchiectasis, ground-glass opacity, or other morphology alone without an ILD (or equivalent) diagnostic attribution. Interstitial findings attributed solely to pulmonary edema, heart failure, fluid overload, or vascular congestion do not support ILD. Findings described only in clinical history, indication, exposure history, or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
