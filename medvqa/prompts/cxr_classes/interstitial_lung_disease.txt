According to the report, the current CXR image(s) shows evidence of interstitial lung disease (ILD) or a diffuse interstitial lung pattern. Supporting findings include ILD presented as a current radiographic diagnosis; an interstitial disease, process, or pattern; diffuse interstitial opacities or increased interstitial markings; diffuse reticular or reticulonodular opacities; non-edematous septal thickening; honeycombing; traction bronchiectasis or bronchiolectasis associated with fibrosis; and diffuse pulmonary fibrosis or fibrosis explicitly attributed to ILD. Ground-glass or micronodular or miliary opacity supports ILD only when characterized as interstitial. A disease name such as UIP, NSIP, IPF, or another fibrotic interstitial pneumonia does not alone establish current radiographic ILD unless the report presents it as a current imaging finding or describes a current interstitial or fibrotic pattern. Focal or apical scarring, isolated bronchiectasis, and airspace disease without an interstitial component do not support ILD, and neither do interstitial findings attributed solely to pulmonary edema, heart failure, fluid overload, or vascular congestion. Base the assessment on the current image; history-, indication-, exposure-, or prior-only mentions do not support current presence, while stable or unchanged ILD findings explicitly present on the current exam remain supportive.
