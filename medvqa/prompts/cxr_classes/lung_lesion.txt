According to the report, the current CXR image(s) shows evidence of one or more discrete nodular or mass-like lesions within the lung parenchyma. Supporting findings include an explicit pulmonary or lung nodule, mass, tumor, neoplasm, coin lesion, pulmonary granuloma, or nodular pulmonary metastasis, whether solitary or multiple, calcified, noncalcified, or cavitary. A nonspecific opacity, consolidation, pneumonia, infiltrate, atelectasis, cavity, cyst, or bulla does not support Lung Lesion unless explicitly described as or associated with a discrete intrapulmonary nodule, mass, tumor, neoplasm, or granuloma. Nodal, hilar without intrapulmonary origin, pleural, mediastinal, osseous, chest-wall, soft-tissue, skin, nipple, device-related, and other extrapulmonary findings are excluded, as are pseudonodules, artifacts, and composite opacities. Lung Lesion is intentionally an intrapulmonary discrete-lesion class and need not accompany every broader thoracic Mass finding or mass-equivalent. Base the assessment on current imaging; history-, indication-, differential-, or prior-only mentions do not support current presence, while a stable or unchanged lesion explicitly present on the current exam remains supportive.
