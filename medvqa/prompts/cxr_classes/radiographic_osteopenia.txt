According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show radiographic osteopenia when they describe osteopenia, osteopenic bones, osseous demineralization, or decreased, reduced, or diminished mineralization or density of bones within the field of view, whether generalized or regional. Osteoporosis or osteoporotic change also supports this finding when described as a current radiographic osseous finding. Do not infer osteopenia from apparent lucency due to imaging technique, or from fractures, compression deformities, kyphosis, scoliosis, degenerative changes, osteophytes, lytic lesions, osteomyelitis, osteonecrosis, or osteoarthritis alone. Findings described only in clinical history, indication, on a prior examination, or by DXA or DEXA results alone do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
