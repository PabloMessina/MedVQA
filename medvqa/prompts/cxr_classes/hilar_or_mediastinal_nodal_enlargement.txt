According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show hilar or mediastinal lymph-node enlargement. Presence is supported by explicit hilar or mediastinal adenopathy, lymphadenopathy, enlarged lymph nodes, nodal enlargement, or pathologically enlarged or prominent nodes. Do not infer nodal enlargement from a hilar, perihilar, or mediastinal mass unless it is identified as adenopathy, enlarged nodes, or a nodal mass or conglomerate. Likewise, do not infer it from malignancy, lymphoma, metastatic disease, granulomatous disease, or a history of cancer without stated nodal enlargement. Hilar fullness, prominence, or enlargement and mediastinal widening qualify only when attributed to adenopathy or nodal enlargement rather than vascular or another non-nodal cause. Calcified lymph nodes do not qualify unless enlargement or adenopathy is also stated. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
