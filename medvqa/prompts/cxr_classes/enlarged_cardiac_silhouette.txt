According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show an enlarged cardiac silhouette. Explicit cardiomegaly or megacardia, an enlarged, dilated, hypertrophied, globular, or massive heart or cardiac silhouette, enlargement of a named cardiac chamber, or an elevated cardiothoracic ratio supports this finding. Borderline cardiac enlargement is a hedged description of enlargement and remains in scope; by contrast, “top normal” or “upper limits of normal” describes a normal size and does not support this finding. A “prominent” cardiac silhouette supports it only when the wording clearly denotes abnormal cardiac enlargement. Do not infer cardiac-silhouette enlargement from CHF, fluid overload, pericardial effusion, mediastinal widening, enlarged cardiomediastinum, aortic unfolding, or pulmonary artery enlargement unless enlargement of the heart or cardiac silhouette is also described. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
