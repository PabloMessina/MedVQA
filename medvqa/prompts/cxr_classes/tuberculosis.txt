According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show evidence of thoracic tuberculosis when current findings are explicitly diagnosed as or attributed to tuberculosis, TB, MTB, Mycobacterium tuberculosis infection, tuberculous disease, tuberculoma, or Koch disease, including pulmonary, primary, progressive primary, post-primary, reactivation, secondary, miliary, endobronchial, nodal, pleural, or empyema manifestations. Explicitly visible residual sequelae attributed to prior, healed, or inactive TB also support the finding, including a Ghon focus or complex, Ranke complex, tuberculous granuloma, or apical or upper-lobe fibrocalcific, fibronodular, or calcific scarring; the attribution and current visibility distinguish these sequelae from history alone. Do not infer tuberculosis from granuloma, cavitation, miliary nodules, consolidation, adenopathy, pleural effusion, empyema, or apical scarring without an explicit TB link, and do not infer it from NTM or MAC unless TB is also stated. Latent TB, exposure, positive PPD or IGRA testing, and history, indication, or prior-only mentions do not support current radiographic presence without a visible TB-attributed manifestation; absent or resolved TB without residual radiographic evidence likewise does not support presence. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
