According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show one or more clavicle fractures. Acute, subacute, healing, healed, old, remote, traumatic, pathologic, iatrogenic, or postoperative clavicular fractures support this finding, including fractures of the midshaft, distal or lateral clavicle, proximal or medial clavicle, or clavicular head. Callus, fracture deformity, displacement, angulation, comminution, nonunion, or malunion also supports the finding when explicitly attributed to a clavicle fracture. Do not infer a clavicle fracture from an isolated fracture of another bone, acromioclavicular separation, soft-tissue injury, nonspecific clavicular contour change, bony destruction, or a lytic lesion without stated fracture. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
