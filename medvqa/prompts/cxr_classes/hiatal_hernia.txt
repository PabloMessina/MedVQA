According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show a hiatal or hiatus hernia. Presence is supported by explicit hiatal or hiatus hernia, including sliding, paraesophageal, rolling, mixed, and type I–IV forms, or by intrathoracic stomach or gastric herniation attributed to a hiatal hernia. A retrocardiac opacity, mass, or air-fluid level supports this finding only when attributed to a hiatal or hiatus hernia. Do not infer hiatal hernia from reflux alone, from an elevated hemidiaphragm or eventration, or from nonspecific intrathoracic gas or bowel-like opacity. Non-hiatal diaphragmatic hernias (e.g., Bochdalek or Morgagni), traumatic diaphragmatic hernia without hiatal wording, and lung tissue herniating beyond the thoracic cage do not support this subtype unless a hiatal hernia is also stated. Exclude expected postoperative anatomy such as gastric pull-up, epiphrenic diverticulum, phrenic ampulla, and other retrocardiac mimics unless a separate current or recurrent hiatal hernia is described. Findings described only in clinical history, as a prior repair, or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
