Do not infer this concept from generic pleural densities, opacities, abnormalities, pleural-based or pleural-parenchymal abnormalities, pleural effusion, empyema, or hemothorax. Isolated fissural thickening supports presence only when explicitly characterized as chronic pleural thickening, scarring, or fibrosis and not attributed to fissural fluid; exclude isolated pleural masses, extrapleural fat, and chest-wall shadows. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR.
