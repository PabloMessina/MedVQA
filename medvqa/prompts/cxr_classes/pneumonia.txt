According to the report, the current CXR image(s) show infection of the lung parenchyma. Presence is supported by pneumonia or an equivalent current pulmonary infection, including infectious pneumonitis, bronchopneumonia, lobar, segmental, multifocal, atypical, or aspiration pneumonia, community-, hospital-, or ventilator-associated pneumonia, Pneumocystis pneumonia, or PNA when it denotes pneumonia, and when a current lung abnormality is attributed to a bacterial, viral, fungal, mycobacterial, or other infectious cause. Hedged current-image attribution such as possible pneumonia or “cannot exclude pneumonia” remains evidence to interpret under the system instructions rather than an exclusion. Do not infer pneumonia from consolidation, airspace opacity, infiltrate, haziness, cavitation, or volume loss without infection attribution; exclude noninfectious processes, idiopathic or fibrotic interstitial pneumonias, organizing pneumonia, and infection confined to the pleura, airways, or bloodstream unless concurrent parenchymal infection is stated. Findings described only in clinical history or only on a prior examination do not establish this finding on the current CXR. Apply the general instructions for hedging, negation, temporality, and current-versus-prior findings.
