According to the report, the current CXR image(s) shows infection of the lung parenchyma. Presence is supported by pneumonia or an equivalent current pulmonary infection, including infectious pneumonitis, bronchopneumonia, lobar, segmental, multifocal, atypical, or aspiration pneumonia, community-, hospital-, or ventilator-associated pneumonia, Pneumocystis pneumonia, or PNA when it denotes pneumonia, and when a current lung abnormality is attributed to a bacterial, viral, fungal, mycobacterial, or other infectious cause. Hedged current-image attribution such as possible pneumonia or “cannot exclude pneumonia” remains evidence to interpret under the system instructions rather than an exclusion. Do not infer pneumonia from consolidation, airspace opacity, infiltrate, haziness, cavitation, or volume loss without infection attribution; exclude noninfectious processes, idiopathic or fibrotic interstitial pneumonias, organizing pneumonia, and infection confined to the pleura, airways, or bloodstream unless concurrent parenchymal infection is stated. Explicit current pneumonia supports a pulmonary opacity, but morphology alone does not establish pneumonia. History-only and prior-only pneumonia do not support current presence. Findings described as absent, cleared, or fully resolved do not support current presence; improving or resolving language is interpreted according to the system-level certainty rules.
